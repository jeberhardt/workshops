Output 2 · Haptic motor

A pager motor needs more current than a pin can give, so a transistor switches it.

GPIO 13 (pin 33) through 1kΩ to an NPN transistor. Motor on the 3.3V rail, with a diode across it.

[Fritzing diagram: haptic motor + transistor on GPIO 13]

from gpiozero import PWMOutputDevice
haptic = PWMOutputDevice(13)

“buzz” plays a three-pulse pattern; “rumble” sets a continuous strength. Patterns are where haptics get expressive: try a heartbeat (two short pulses, a pause) or a slow swell. Never wire the motor straight to a GPIO pin — it can draw far more than the pin is rated for. The diode soaks up the voltage spike when the motor switches off. If your motors came on breakout boards with a driver, you can skip the transistor and connect the board’s signal pin directly. [Confirm part numbers for the transistor and diode in the kit.]
