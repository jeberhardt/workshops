Outputs

01 · LIGHT

LEDs

Three colour LEDs on one wire. The sketch sets each one’s colour and brightness.

02 · SOUND

Buzzer

A tiny speaker that plays tones. The sketch picks the pitch and when it sounds.

Inputs let the audience talk to the sketch; outputs let the piece answer in the room, not just on screen. The LEDs are three WS2812 NeoPixels chained together: 5V, ground, and one data line through a 330Ω resistor and a logic level converter (3.3V to 5V) to GPIO 10 (SPI MOSI), driven by the adafruit-circuitpython-neopixel-spi library, which works on every Pi from the 3 to the 5 without sudo. The buzzer is a small passive buzzer driven through a PN2222A transistor from GPIO 13, with a flyback diode across it, because it draws more current than a GPIO pin should supply. gpiozero's TonalBuzzer plays notes; it's loudest around 2 to 2.7 kHz.
