More inputs to explore

Arcade button

Big, satisfying, built to be mashed

Rotary encoder

A knob that turns forever, no end stops

Capacitive touch

Turns foil, metal or fruit into touch pads

PIR motion

Did someone move nearby? Yes or no

Time-of-flight

Laser distance: narrower and steadier than ultrasonic

Light sensor

Shadows, flashlights, day turning to night

Microphone

Claps, voices, the roar of a crowd

Accelerometer

Tilt, shake and spin an object

Force sensor

How hard someone presses, sits or stands

Camera

Bodies, faces and colour, right in p5.js

Weather

Temperature and humidity as material

Visitors’ phones

A web page that joins the same WebSocket

All of these follow the pattern from today: read it in Python, send a message, react in p5.js. Analog ones (light, force) go through the MCP3008 like the slider. Time-of-flight, accelerometer, capacitive touch (MPR121) and weather (BME280) are usually I2C boards. The camera and a USB microphone skip the bridge entirely: p5.js can read them directly with createCapture() and p5.sound.
