Or skip the browser: send the same messages to TouchDesigner, Unity or Processing.

Not hands-on, just possibilities. Same network: serve the bridge on 0.0.0.0 and point the sketch at ws://[pi-ip]:8765 instead of localhost. Phones: the Pi serves the page; the venue network must let devices see each other, so bring your own router. Anywhere: the Pi and the remote display both connect outward to a relay — a small hosted WebSocket server, an MQTT broker (many support browsers over secure WebSockets), or a private network like Tailscale. Because both sides connect out, nobody touches the venue’s router. Expect some delay, use encrypted connections (wss://), and don’t rely on public brokers for anything private. TouchDesigner, Unity and Processing usually take the messages as OSC.
