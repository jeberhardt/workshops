Inputs

01 · ON / OFF

Button

Pressed or not. The simplest thing a visitor can do.

02 · DISTANCE

Proximity sensor

How close someone is, many times a second.

03 · ANALOG

Light sensor

How much light falls on it, smoothly from dark to bright.

Hold up each part as you name it. Each one adds a kind of data: the button is a single on/off, the HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensor turns a person's distance into a number, and the photoresistor (LDR) gives a continuous analog value. The Pi has no analog inputs, so the LDR goes through a 10kΩ voltage divider into an ADS1115 converter over I2C (GPIO 2 and 3). In an installation, a shadow or a hand over the LDR becomes the interaction.
