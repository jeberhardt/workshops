Why a Raspberry Pi?

01

Hides inside the work

Small enough to live in a plinth, a box or behind a wall.

02

Boots into your sketch

Power on and the piece starts. Nobody has to log in.

03

Reads the real world

Its GPIO pins take buttons, knobs and sensors directly.

A laptop works too, until it’s left alone in a public space all night.

Frame the Pi as installation hardware, not a hobby toy. The point is a piece that runs unattended at Nuit Blanche or a brand activation.
